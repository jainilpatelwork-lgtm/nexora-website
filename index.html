Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

Every job is different, so we give you a free quote first. You'll know the price before any work starts, and there are no hidden fees.

Do you come to my home or business?

Yes. We offer on-site visits across York Region and the GTA. Many problems can also be fixed remotely, which is often faster.

Is remote support safe?

Yes. We use secure remote-access software that only works while you're watching and have given permission. You can end the session at any time.

Do I need to buy equipment from you?

No. We'll recommend what you need, and you can buy it wherever you like. We charge only for our work.

What about my files and personal information?

We recommend backing up important files before any repair, and we can do the backup for you. We never keep, share or look through your personal information.

How do I pay?

Interac e-Transfer or cash, after the work is done. You'll receive an invoice for your records.
